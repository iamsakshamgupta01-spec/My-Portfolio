About

I started out breaking things in a browser console and never really stopped. These days that curiosity has turned into a habit of building things properly — readable code, layouts that hold up on any screen, and details that most people won't consciously notice but will feel.

I work mostly with React and vanilla JavaScript, and I care about the CSS as much as the logic. My favourite projects are the ones where the brief leaves room to make a few real decisions.
